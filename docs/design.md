## How was the scope picked?

We used the official PFT first-floor plan to map out the classrooms and outline the area around the Commons. We picked this section because it has a mix of classrooms, computer labs, Panera, and the Commons, where students already spend time. Focusing on one area keeps the project manageable while we work on search, room selection, and department highlighting.

## Palette

- **LSU Purple (`#461D7C`):** Controls, outlines, and headings.
- **LSU Gold (`#FDD023`):** Selected rooms and the Selected tag only.
- **Lavender (`#B9A4DA`):** Department highlights.
- **Ink (`#1C1526`):** Text.
